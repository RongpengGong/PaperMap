# PaperMap 0.2.0（2026-11-26）

[English below](#english)

这一版的重点：**粘贴编号就能导入**、**AI 智能体小队**、**周报 / 月报回顾**、**图片库筛选和参考图**，以及更顺手的多语言。

## 新功能

### 粘贴 DOI / PMID 直接导入
* 侧边栏「导入 PDF」旁新增 **#** 按钮；在任意页面非输入框处按 ⌘V 也会打开。
* 支持 DOI、PubMed ID、PMC ID、arXiv 编号和它们的链接，一次可以贴一串。
* 有合法开放获取全文的（PMC 官方仓库、OpenAlex、arXiv）自动下载并导入；没有的放进「新文献 → 待下载」，之后拖进 PDF 会按 DOI 自动对上。已在库里的只提示，不会重复导入。

### AI 智能体小队
* 把大一点的活交给一组分工的智能体：**课题综述**、**证据核对**、**新文献分诊**，以及论文对话里的**单篇深读**。也可以在任意页面的「问 AI」里切到「小队」模式。
* 队长拆任务，成员并行检索和精读，质疑员挑毛病，执笔员写带出处的报告；过程实时可见，可以随时追加指令。
* 小队只读：摘录、判断、缺口、收录都作为提议交给你确认。编造的原句会被拒绝，没有出处的句子会被删掉。
* 每个角色可以用不同的模型，开始前估算费用并设上限；每两周可以让一个模型参考公开排行榜和你的使用记录，建议角色搭配，由你逐项采纳。

### 回顾：今天、周报、月报
* 首页「今天做了什么」：当天的摘录、阅读、笔记和收藏。
* 周报 / 月报：读了什么、新证据、**值得追问的问题**（未补的缺口、相互矛盾的证据、笔记里的疑问）、阅读积压、下周打算；月报还会回访旧判断、提示「可以写成段落了」。
* 可选一段 AI 小结（每条编号回链），一键生成**组会大纲**。

### 图片库与参考图
* 按**图型**筛选：UMAP、空间分布图、热图、火山图、生存曲线、染色、示意图等 20 类（按图注判断，不花模型费用）。
* 按**论文**筛选：星标、用途标签（如「作图参考」）、分组、学科；也可以搜图注、只看正文图。滚动自动加载更多。
* 大图里「**收藏为参考图**」，写一句为什么收藏；图型判断错了点一下改正。图片库顶部有「★ 我的参考图」。
* 在大图里可以直接「问 AI」讨论这张图。

### 数据资源筛选
* 来源（GEO、SRA、EGA……）、用途（本研究产生 / 引用的公开数据）、论文范围都改成点一下就能筛的分类，数量随其他筛选变化。
* 新增搜索框：登录号、题目、作者、期刊、上下文。CSV 导出沿用当前筛选。

### 语言
* 界面语言没选过时**跟随系统**；设置里新增「跟随系统」。
* 卡片语言新增**繁體中文**和**德语**，默认跟随界面语言。
* 翻译目标语言新增繁体、德、日、韩、法、西。
* 改了卡片语言后，「设置 → 论文处理 → 已有卡片的语言」会显示有几张需要重新生成和预计费用；确认后在后台逐张重做，手动改过的卡片跳过，失败的保留原卡片，关掉应用后会接着做。

### 其他
* 主模型可以用 **Google Antigravity 订阅**（本机官方 CLI 登录）。
* 论文卡片以卡片块显示，卡片右键可「问 AI」；笔记和学科等页面也以卡片显示。
* 全文翻译完成前核对 PDF 文字覆盖，并能检查已有译文的漏译、只补缺的段落。
* 修正：PDF 表格里参考文献栏之后的正文不再丢失。

## 更流畅
* 图片库页面体积从约 650 KB 降到约 130 KB，生成页面从约 230 毫秒降到约 6 毫秒；缩略图可以缓存。
* 去掉列表和详情工具栏上没有视觉效果的背景模糊，滚动更顺。
* 窗口隐藏时暂停后台刷新。
* 退出应用改为有序停止。

## 升级
下载 `PaperMap-macOS-AppleSilicon.zip`，解压后替换「应用程序」里的 PaperMap.app。你的文献库、设置和笔记在 `~/PaperMap`，不受影响。已有设置里选定的语言保持不变。

# PaperMap 0.2.0 (2026-11-26)

Highlights: **add papers by pasting an ID**, **AI agent squads**, **weekly / monthly recaps**, **figure-library filters and reference figures**, and better language handling.

## New

### Paste a DOI / PMID to import
* A **#** button next to "Import PDF" (or press ⌘V outside a text box on any page).
* DOIs, PubMed IDs, PMC IDs, arXiv IDs and links to them, a whole list at once.
* Papers with a legal open-access PDF (PMC's official archive, OpenAlex, arXiv) are downloaded and imported; the rest wait under New papers → To download and match a PDF you drag in later by DOI. Papers already in the library are reported, not imported twice.

### AI agent squads
* Hand larger jobs to a team: **topic review**, **evidence check**, **new-paper triage**, and **deep reading** from a paper's AI chat; or switch any page's "Ask AI" drawer to squad mode.
* A lead plans the task board, members search and read in parallel, a critic challenges, a writer drafts a sourced report – visible live, and you can add instructions at any time.
* Read-only: quotes, judgements, gaps and papers to add come back as proposals for you to accept. Invented quotes are rejected and unsourced sentences removed.
* A model per role, a cost estimate and cap before starting, and an optional fortnightly review that suggests role assignments from public rankings and your usage, adopted role by role.

### Recaps: today, weekly, monthly
* "What I did today" on the home page.
* Weekly / monthly reports: reading, new evidence, **questions worth pursuing** (open gaps, conflicting evidence, questions in your notes), reading backlog, next week's plan; monthly reports revisit old judgements and point out topics ready to write up.
* Optional AI summary with numbered links back, and a one-click **lab-meeting outline**.

### Figure library and reference figures
* Filter by **plot type** – UMAP, spatial maps, heatmaps, volcano plots, survival curves, staining, schematics and more (20 types, guessed from the legend, free).
* Filter by **papers** – starred, purpose tags such as "figure reference", groups, fields – search legends, or show main figures only. More load as you scroll.
* **Keep as reference** in the figure viewer, with a note on why; correct a wrong plot type with one click. "★ My reference figures" at the top of the gallery.
* Ask AI about a figure right in the viewer.

### Data resource filters
* Repository, use (generated / reused) and paper scope are clickable chips with live counts.
* New search across accessions, titles, authors, journals and context; the CSV export keeps the filters.

### Languages
* The interface **follows the system** until you choose a language; a "Follow system" option was added.
* Cards can now be written in **Traditional Chinese** and **German**, following the interface language by default.
* New translation targets: Traditional Chinese, German, Japanese, Korean, French, Spanish.
* After changing the card language, Settings → Paper processing → "Language of existing cards" shows how many cards to regenerate and the cost; confirm to redo them in the background (hand-edited cards are skipped, failures keep the old card, and it resumes after a restart).

### Other
* **Google Antigravity subscription** as a main model (signed in through the official local CLI).
* Paper cards shown as tiles, with "Ask AI" in the card's right-click menu; notes and field pages shown as cards too.
* Full-text translations check PDF text coverage before finishing; existing translations can be audited for omissions and only missing paragraphs patched.
* Fix: article text after reference columns in PDF tables is no longer lost.

## Smoother
* The figure library page went from about 650 KB to 130 KB and is generated in about 6 ms instead of 230 ms; thumbnails are cacheable.
* No more needless background blur on the list and detail toolbars, for smoother scrolling.
* Background refresh pauses while the window is hidden.
* Quitting the app now stops it in an orderly way.

## Upgrade
Download `PaperMap-macOS-AppleSilicon.zip`, unzip it and replace PaperMap.app in Applications. Your library, settings and notes live in `~/PaperMap` and are not affected. Languages you already chose stay as they are.
